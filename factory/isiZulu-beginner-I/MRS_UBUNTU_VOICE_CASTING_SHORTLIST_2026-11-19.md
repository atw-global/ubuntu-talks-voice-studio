## Native / branded authenticity gate
- Listen blind to 15–25 seconds of each paid-tier candidate reading **identical English-only guidance**.
- Evaluate human-like cadence, warm authority, consonant clarity, pause rhythm, emphasis, non-robotic emotional expression, and cultural respect.
- Distinguish synthetic English delivery from native isiZulu pronunciation. A South African-sounding English female TTS voice does not confer native isiZulu competence.
- No public/commercial voice files until paid-tier commercial-output terms and consent/licensing are documented.
- Qualified reviewer and course owner approve the final Mrs Ubuntu cast separately from Thando and Themba's approved isiZulu models.
