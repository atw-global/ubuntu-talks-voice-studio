Your first 30-second challenge

Can you say this aloud?

Sawubona — Hello (one person)
Sanibonani — Hello (a group)
Unjani? — How are you?
Ngiyaphila — I am fine.

Greet one person, ask how they are, answer aloud and then switch to greeting a group—without reading the model.

How you will learn

01 · Hear and notice

Listen to authentic-sounding speech patterns and focus on the pronunciation, rhythm and social context.

02 · Imitate and retrieve

Repeat spoken models, transform phrases and recall them without depending on written text.

03 · Converse and assess

Use guided exchanges, situation changes and a Can-Speak challenge to check progress.
